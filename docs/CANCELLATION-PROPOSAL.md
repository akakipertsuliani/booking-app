# Proposed cancellation rules — owner review required

These are a simple starting proposal for a future service, not active customer terms.

- No online prepayment or deposit at initial launch; pay the agreed total at the shop.
- Allow cancellation or rescheduling at any time before the appointment, without a fee.
  Ask customers to give 24 hours' notice when possible, without making it a penalty.
- Do not charge no-show fees in the first release. Repeated no-shows can be handled
  manually and fairly; do not silently create charges.
- If the barber cancels, offer rescheduling or cancel without cost.
- If prepaid services are introduced later, propose a full refund for a cancellation
  before service begins or a business cancellation, returned using the original method
  without an extra refund fee. Confirm the processing time and statutory rights first.
- Service-quality complaints need a clear contact and fair review rather than a blanket
  "no refunds" rule.

Before accepting real bookings, a Georgian lawyer should review the exact model,
applicable consumer withdrawal rules and exceptions, early-performance requests,
tax-inclusive pricing, treatment of deposits, complaint handling, and Georgian-language
disclosures. Business policy cannot override mandatory consumer rights.

Current customer page /refunds deliberately describes only actual behavior:
no payments, deposits, appointment reservations or cancellation charges.
Do not publish this proposal as an agreed policy until the owner has approved it.
